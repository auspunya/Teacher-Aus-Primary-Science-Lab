🧠 Big Idea (Understanding)

“Everyday tools are complex machines created by combining simple machines such as gears, levers, wheels and axles to function efficiently.”

Understanding how the parts of a tool work together helps us improve its sustainability, safety, cost-effectiveness and environmental impact.
