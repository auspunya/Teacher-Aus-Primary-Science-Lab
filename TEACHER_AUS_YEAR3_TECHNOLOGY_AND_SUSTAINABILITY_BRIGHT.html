🧠 Big Idea (Understanding)

“Simple machines make work easier by changing the direction or magnitude of the force applied.”

Pupils investigate how a pulley helps lift or move loads, use a model to describe how a fixed pulley works, identify everyday applications, and create and explain a functional pulley model.
