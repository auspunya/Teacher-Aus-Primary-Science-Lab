🧠 Big Idea (Understanding)

“Understanding and combining basic 2D and 3D shapes enables us to design, construct, and explain the functions of objects and structures in everyday life.”

Pupils identify shapes around them, combine shapes to construct useful objects, and explain how shape properties influence their functions.
