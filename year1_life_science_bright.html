BODY SYSTEM SPOTLIGHT

🍎 Digestive System

The digestive system breaks food into smaller parts so the body can use nutrients.

🔍 Key parts

Mouth, food pipe, stomach and intestines.

🌟 Try this

Chew a bite of food carefully and notice how the mouth starts digestion. Never conduct activities with unsafe foods or choking hazards.

💬 Remember

The body systems work together. Food gives the body nutrients and energy for growth and activity.
