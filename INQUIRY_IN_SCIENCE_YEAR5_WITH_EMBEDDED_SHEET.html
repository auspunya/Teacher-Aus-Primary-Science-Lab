3Investigation Lab

Choose an investigation route. Follow the guided steps first, then use the explorer prompts to plan your own fair test.

🔎 Explorer mode

Choose one factor to investigate: paper-plane wing shape, or quantity of glycerine in a soap-bubble mixture. Decide what you will change, what you will measure, and what must stay the same. Record your plan before testing.

Safety and fairness: follow your teacher’s instructions; use clear space for paper planes and avoid tasting or splashing bubble solution.

★Teacher Resources

📘 Lesson idea

Use the skill cards as a short introduction, then guide pupils through a paper-plane or soap-bubble investigation.

📝 Question prompts

Ask pupils to explain what they observed, identify variables, record measurements and support conclusions with evidence.

📊 Assessment focus

Listen for clear links between the factor changed, the measurement recorded and the conclusion drawn from results.
