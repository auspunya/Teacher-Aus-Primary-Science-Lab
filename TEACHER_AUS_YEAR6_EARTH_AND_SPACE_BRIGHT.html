🧠 Big Idea (Understanding)

Understanding solar and lunar eclipses helps us explain natural phenomena and recognise the importance of protecting our eyes during solar-eclipse observations. Understanding galaxies helps us recognise Earth’s place in the universe, appreciate the vastness of space, and understand that our Solar System is only a small part of the Milky Way galaxy.
