3Investigation Lab

Choose a guided activity, then use Explorer Mode to suggest your own observation or question. Follow your teacher’s instructions and treat living things gently.

🔎 Explorer Mode

Choose a question to explore: What features can you observe on a leaf? How can objects be grouped by shared features? What information can a class fruit chart show?

Safety and care: use science apparatus as your teacher demonstrates. If observing a grasshopper, keep it briefly in a suitable ventilated container and release it carefully after observation. Do not harm or keep it unnecessarily.

★Teacher Resources

📘 Lesson idea

Use the skill cards as a short introduction, then guide pupils through a plant observation, fruit survey or grasshopper observation.

📝 Question prompts

Ask pupils to explain what they observed, identify variables, record measurements and support conclusions with evidence.

📊 Assessment focus

Listen for clear links between the factor changed, the measurement recorded and the conclusion drawn from results.
