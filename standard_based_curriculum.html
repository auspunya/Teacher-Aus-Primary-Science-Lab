💡 Understand the Curriculum Standards

A clear guide to Content Standard, Learning Standard, Performance Standard and Performance Level.

🎯
WHAT TO LEARN

1. Content Standard (CS)

The broad area of knowledge, skills or values that pupils are expected to develop. It describes the main learning focus for a topic or unit.

Example: A topic may focus on understanding living things and their needs.

Teacher use: Read the CS first to understand the overall learning direction before planning a lesson.

🔎
WHAT PUPILS SHOULD ACHIEVE

2. Learning Standard (LS)

A more specific learning outcome that explains what pupils should be able to demonstrate in relation to the Content Standard.

Example: Pupils identify the basic needs of a living thing, as stated in the relevant DSKP.

Teacher use: Use the LS to write lesson objectives, choose activities and decide what evidence to collect.

📊
HOW WELL LEARNING IS SHOWN

3. Performance Standard (PS)

The reference criteria in the DSKP used to describe pupils’ level of mastery of the learning being assessed. It helps teachers make consistent judgements from evidence.

Teacher use: Refer to the relevant PS when assessing a pupil; consider evidence from questioning, observation, written work and practical tasks.

📈
LEVEL OF MASTERY

4. Performance Level (PL) / Tahap Penguasaan (TP)

A level assigned to describe the pupil’s demonstrated mastery against the Performance Standard. In Malaysian KSSR classroom-based assessment (PBD), the levels are generally reported as TP1 to TP6. Always use the descriptors for the specific subject and learning area in the applicable DSKP; the wording and expectations can differ by standard.

TP1

Know

Shows basic knowledge of the relevant content, according to the DSKP descriptor.

TP2

Understand

Shows understanding of the content or skill, according to the relevant descriptor.

TP3

Apply

Uses knowledge or skills in a familiar situation, where specified by the descriptor.

TP4

Analyse / Use skills

Demonstrates a more developed level of performance as described in the subject-specific DSKP.

TP5

Evaluate / Solve

Demonstrates a high level of mastery, such as making judgements or solving problems when required by the descriptor.

TP6

Create / Innovate

Demonstrates the highest level described in the relevant DSKP, which may include creativity, innovation or exemplary application.

Important: The short labels above are a general learning aid, not universal official definitions of TP1–TP6. Do not assign a TP simply by matching it to a Bloom’s Taxonomy verb. Use the exact Performance Standard descriptor in the Science DSKP and the evidence collected from the pupil.

🧠 Standards at a glance

Use the three standards together when planning teaching, learning and assessment.

01 · PLAN

Content Standard

Identifies the knowledge, skills and values pupils are expected to acquire.

02 · TEACH

Learning Standard

Describes the specific learning outcomes that guide lessons and learning activities.

03 · ASSESS

Performance Standard

Provides criteria for interpreting pupils' demonstrated levels of mastery.
